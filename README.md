# Stock Markets Analytics Zoomcamp 2026

Python-based stock-market analysis covering data collection, statistical analysis, indicators, dashboards, and trading-oriented research.

## Coursework

- [Module 1: Introduction and Data Sources](01-intro-and-data-sources/README.md) — environment setup, reproducible Homework 1 calculations, answers, and findings.
- [Module 2: DataFrame Analysis](02-dataframe-analysis/README.md) — IPO web scraping, Sharpe ratio analysis, holding horizon evaluation, and RSI oversold backtest for Homework 2.
- [Module 3: Time Series Modeling](03-modeling/README.md) — seasonal calendar dummy variables, manual hand-crafted rules, unique ML predictive contributions, and Decision Tree hyperparameter tuning for Homework 3.
- [Module 4: Trading Strategy and Simulation](04-trading-strategy-and-simulation/README.md) — feature reduction impact on CAGR, Random Forest probability threshold optimization, growth target redefinition, and strategy design for Homework 4.
- [Module 5: Deployment and Automation](05-deployment-and-automation/README.md) — three distinct, structured stock research proposals (local-market export pass-through, alternative gaming telemetry, and supply-chain cointegrated semiconductor pairs) for Homework 5.
